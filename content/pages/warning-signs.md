## Go to a vet straight away if your dog

- strains or squats to pee but passes little or no urine
- keeps trying to pee and seems restless or uncomfortable
- has a painful or tense belly
- is vomiting, very quiet or off their food *and* is struggling to pee

Phone ahead so the clinic can get ready. Out of hours, your own vet's answerphone will give the emergency number, or you can find your nearest emergency clinic through [Vets Now](https://www.vets-now.com).

## Book a vet appointment soon if you notice

- blood in the urine (pink, red or brown pee)
- peeing more often than usual, in small amounts
- accidents indoors from a dog who's normally clean
- licking around the genitals more than usual
- arching the back or crying out while peeing
- pee that smells unusually strong

These signs don't always mean stones. Infections cause many of the same symptoms. But in a Dalmatian, it's always worth getting them checked.

## Why male Dalmatians are most at risk

Males have a long, narrow urethra (the tube that carries urine out of the body), which passes through a small bone in the penis. A stone that would pass easily in a female can get stuck there and block the flow of urine completely.

## What to tell your vet

- **That your dog is a Dalmatian.** Dalmatians are prone to **urate** stones, which are less common in other breeds, so it changes what your vet looks for.
- **Urate stones often don't show up on a standard X-ray.** They are usually seen with ultrasound, so a clear X-ray doesn't rule them out.
- **What your dog eats,** including treats and supplements. Our [treats guide](/treats) lists the ones to watch.

It's normal to find urate *crystals* in a Dalmatian's urine. Crystals aren't stones, and on their own they aren't a reason to panic, but tell your vet if they're found alongside any of the signs above.

## Reducing the risk

You can greatly reduce the risk with a [low-purine diet](/purine-guide), [plenty of water](/water-and-urine) and regular chances to pee. See our [three rules](/three-rules) for the basics.
