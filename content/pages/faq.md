## What are the signs of urinary stones?

Watch out for blood in the urine, urinating often, pain when urinating, not urinating at all, or arching the back while urinating. See the full list on our [warning signs page](/warning-signs).

## What should I do if I think my dog has urinary stones?

**See your vet straight away.** A blocked bladder is an emergency, especially in male dogs. Read our [warning signs page](/warning-signs) for what to look out for.

## Do all Dalmatians get stones?

No. Every traditional Dalmatian has high uric acid, but many never form stones. A low-purine diet and plenty of water reduce the risk. Read about the gene behind it, and low uric acid Dalmatians, on our [DNA testing page](/dna-testing).

## Can my Dalmatian have liver treats?

It's best not to. Liver and other organ meats are among the highest-purine foods there are. See our [treats guide](/treats) for better options.
