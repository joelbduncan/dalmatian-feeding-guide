## Why this matters

Dalmatians' livers have trouble breaking down uric acid. It can build up in the blood ([hyperuricemia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperuricemia)), causing gout, or be passed in high concentrations into the urine, where it forms kidney and bladder stones.

These problems are most common in middle-aged males. The risk can be greatly reduced with a **low-purine diet**, regular exercise and plenty of fresh water.
