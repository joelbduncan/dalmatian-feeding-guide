## A puppy with a problem

I started this site shortly after we brought home our first Dalmatian puppy, Zeus. It wasn't long before he began having trouble urinating.

His first visit to the vet led to a misdiagnosis, and money spent on treatment that didn't help. It was only after we'd done our own research and raised it with the vet that he got the right treatment.

## Getting his stones under control

Zeus turned out to be very prone to urinary stones. After a lot of reading, we managed to get them under control through his diet, and he had no further problems for the rest of his life.

## Why this site exists

Everything we'd learned was scattered across forums, old pamphlets and research papers. I wanted to bring it together in one place that was easy to search and easy to understand, so other owners wouldn't have to piece it together the way we did.

I built this site in 2014, when Zeus was only a few months old. I've run and updated it ever since, and it has always been free and ad-free.

> If something doesn't seem right with your Dalmatian, trust your instincts. Tell your vet your dog is a Dalmatian and ask about urate stones. Our [warning signs page](/warning-signs) has what to look out for.
