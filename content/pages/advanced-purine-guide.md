**How to read this table:** *Total purines* is the amount of uric acid (in mg) produced per 100 g of the food. *Min* and *max* show the measured range where one is known. *Nutrient density* is purines per megajoule of energy, which is useful for comparing foods that are eaten in very different amounts.
