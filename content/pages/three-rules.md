## A healthy diet low in purines

It's a misconception that low purine means low protein. Dalmatians need a relatively high-protein diet, but because of their unusual uric acid metabolism, that protein must be good quality and low in purines.

You'll find suitable low-purine protein sources in our [purine guide](/purine-guide), and complete foods in our [dry food guide](/dry-food-guide).

## Frequent, regular exercise

Dalmatians are large dogs with excellent endurance and stamina. They need at least an hour of exercise once, and preferably twice, a day, including plenty of off-lead running and play.

Under-exercised Dalmatians can suffer from stress, hyperactivity and, in extreme cases, aggression.

## Plenty of fresh water

Dalmatians should always have clean, fresh water to drink, and regular access to the outdoors so they can urinate often. Dilute urine is one of the best defences against stones.
