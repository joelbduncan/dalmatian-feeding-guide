**Available as:** dry.

**How it works:** selected low-purine proteins, reduced overall protein and a formula designed to keep urine at the right pH.

**How long to feed it:** Royal Canin recommends up to 6 months to reduce urate stone formation, and lifelong feeding where uric acid metabolism is permanently affected, as in Dalmatians. Follow your vet's advice.
