summary: A veterinary diet for dogs prone to urate and cystine stones. It's low in purines and protein, and helps push urine towards alkaline, which makes urate less likely to crystallise.
highlight: Vet prescription only
---

**Available as:** dry and wet (canned).

**How it works:** controlled, lower protein with reduced purines means less uric acid in the urine. Extra buffering raises urine pH, and sodium is kept low.

**Good to know:** dogs on this diet often have very dilute urine (specific gravity below 1.020) and low blood urea on tests. That's expected, and your vet will use these results to check the diet is working.
