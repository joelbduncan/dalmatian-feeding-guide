**Composition:** Brown rice (37%), lamb (30%) (lamb meat meal, lamb gravy), barley (10%), potato protein (7%), rapeseed oil (6%), whole linseed (4%), sugar beet pulp (2%), alfalfa (0.8%), minerals (includes yucca extract 200 mg/kg, marigold meal 50 mg/kg, rosemary extract 5 mg/kg), seaweed (0.5%), glucosamine (0.1%), chondroitin sulphate (0.1%).

**Analytical constituents:** Protein 26%, crude fibre 4%, oils and fats 12%, crude ash 8%, moisture 10%.

**Feeding guide (approx. per day):** Large dogs (25–45 kg): 335–650 g. Giant dogs (45–70 kg): 650–1,100 g.
